Multiple Endings / 'the

4Following
Go To

All spoilers are unmarked for each of the endings. Please proceed with caution. You Have Been Warned.

There are many endings to get in this ROM hack; 5 in the original, and many more as of the 2025 update, some of which are hard to get and few of which make much sense.

NOTE: None of these endings have official names and are just fan-picked ones based on what goes on in these endings.

Original endings

Getting caught

If Mario gets caught by Ghost Peach, the game seemingly resets. However, when the player re-enters the level again, the Forest appears to be frozen over, with ghostly images of Mario appearing and disappearing. There are two outcomes (three as of the update) that can happen from here:
  • "Ghost Mario" ending: If Mario keeps going through the level, eventually, he arrives at the large hill midway through the Forest again, except it and the surrounding area are covered with blood. Mario enters a door, which wasn't there previously, and arrives at a treehouse with his corpse in the middle of the room. He then leaves and finds himself walking around in an underwater void, before coming across his ghost. At this point, the game resets for real.
  • "Burning Forest" ending: Alternatively, there's a Mario silhouette that you can position Mario in, which causes a message reading "the heart" to appear. At this point, walking back towards the start and pressing Start while on the first bridge causes it to break. This takes you back to what appears to be another reset, but entering the level reveals the Forest is on fire this time, while some message boxes say what appear to be lines of poetry. Inputting a button code takes you to a white area with various messages relating to something that happened on July 31st, 1992.

Escapingnote Drinking the milk in the "Ghost Mario" route after meeting the Oracle for the first time allows you free access to one of these endings without needing to escape Ghost Peach

If Mario successfully evades Ghost Peach, the player reappears in the Forest controlling Peach. At this point, there are two outcomes (four as of the update) for the following event:
  • "I WATCH" ending: If Peach wanders to the end, she eventually comes across a pit, but is forced to walk towards it against her will while a ghostly silhouette watches on and taunts her. After she falls, the game seemingly resets and the player is Mario once again, but this time the Forest has a reddish hue. As Mario proceeds forward, the screen blanks out and then reappears with him standing before a pool of blood while a dramatic Scare Chord sounds out. The game becomes softlockednote at least, in the original build at this point, but if the player holds down the R button, the screen briefly scrolls over to a ghoulish Peach with "I WATCH" written in blood below her.
    • After getting the "Research Book" ending, this ending is rendered permanently inaccessible. The dialogue leading up to the last pit in Peach's normal level is gone, along with the spirits and darkness that once surrounded it, and jumping into the pit simply restarts the game.

  • "Treehouse" ending: If Peach killed all of the Rexes, the disembodied voice suggests that she should "go back and show THEM". If she does so, however, part of a bridge collapses and plunges her into the water, at which point the game abruptly and seemingly resets. (If she continues forward instead, the previous ending with the pit will play.) However, when the player re-enters the level, Mario is shown falling through a hole and eventually onto a platform in the trees in an underwater-like environment. Mario is tasked by the disembodied voice with removing weeds and gathering wood to build the treehouse/cabin seen in the first ending above; each time he completes one part of the task, the disembodied voice is delighted and he is shown falling through the hole again. When the treehouse is complete, Mario is shown falling one more time, but as he falls through the hole in the roof, the screen cuts to black and the game softlocks, forcing the player to reset manually. It's slightly implied that this ending takes place sometime before the game, as the player can come across the cabin with Mario's corpse in it prior to this ending. In addition, the above scene of events happens again in the secret forest ending, only this time there is a second Mario standing in the place you'd land.

Miscellaneous

  • "Idle" ending: Overlapping with Non-Standard Game Over; after entering the level for the first time, if you don't move or press any buttons, a message pops up every 15 or so seconds. After the third such message, the game freezes and crashes the emulator. In the update, the game simply restarts.
  • Halo "ending": Another variation on a Non-Standard Game Over: doing nothing when tasked with pulling up weeds in the tree house reveals that there's still a stage timer running in the background, and if it runs out, you die as you would in a real, regular Mario game. You're then taken back to the map screen, where your map icon Mario has a halo above him.

  • "Pink Forest" ending: In the original release, the player needs to hack the game to access a hidden area, which is more Bloodier and Gorier than what is notably present in the game already, whereas the update allows you to use START on the first bridge to make it collapse and warp you here:
    • Original version: Approaching the giant Peach demon normally causes a voice to remark that something fell, with the emulator crashing afterwards. However, using L to slip by before the dialogue can trigger has Mario plummet into the same pit as the "Treehouse" ending, before inexplicably landing on another Mario inside.
    • Update version: After leaving the pink forest, Mario is lost in his thoughts as he wanders through an outdoor area and a waterlogged cave in search for a key. However, upon picking it up and trying to leave, a ghost version of him catches him at the top and takes the key, daring Mario to find him to consider himself worthy of holding the key. After the ghost is gone and Mario goes back into the lake's depths, the game resets.

Update endings

General

  • "Oracle" endings: Each of these sequences trigger automatically when one of the four original endings is found. In each one, Mario wanders into a house that's home to a mysterious oracle who drops cryptic hints for him to look into. After the second time, big Marios start popping up in the forest to give cryptic dialogue, and come the fourth time, the Oracle is all but gone, having left since the third ending. After Mario leaves, he is met by Peach, who holds him in a gentle hug. While wishing the moment could last longer, she says she needs to get going, leaving with the parting words "I'll miss you.".
  • "Judgement" ending: Walking back into a randomly-decided hidden wall at the start and doing a Pacifist Run in Peach's side of the forest leads to her needing to backtrack, with Mario next seen standing in a small pit and being judged by unseen beings. On his way through a graveyard, he eventually stumbles into a tower-like building where he can collect one of the colors. Right after, a giant Mario head appears in the background, and after some monologuing, the game restarts.

Getting caught

  • "In the Corner" ending: Entering a hidden passage in the treehouse of the "Ghost Mario" path leads Mario into a tall tower, all while a voice tries to dissuade him from climbing any higher up there. Eventually, he manages to reach the top, greeted by two huge eyes peering through windows, and a voice mentioning that "you've" been sitting in a corner for hours, before declaring that "There's nothing here for you." before the game resets.
    • Coming here after getting the updated "Pink Forest" ending has the ghost Mario greet you at the top and give you the key for the red door as promised.

Escaping

  • "Research Book" ending: Getting this ending requires you to do a Pacifist Run of Peach's level up to 4 times, by not killing a single Rex and getting the "I WATCH" ending repeatedly. After the fourth time, Peach suddenly appears in Mario's place, who declares she'll find a way to fix things before leaping into the blood waters and into another corner of the forest. In spite of a white figure trying to dissuade her from going onwards, she eventually enters a "building" made up of loose paper and collects the research book, which remains accessible from the inventory afterwards. Once she does so, the room suddenly shakes before the game resets.
  • "Summit" ending: Leaving the treehouse before gathering wood for the roof has Mario venture down into a hidden cave system before emerging into an open area with the moon hanging in the background. He can wander around in this space until he reaches a drop at the furthest end of the area, and jumping down causes the game to reset.

True Endings

  • "Final" ending: After collecting all eight colors, solving a cryptic puzzle with them at the summit and wandering through collapsing rooms tied to the forest, Mario and Peach both come across the alien lifeform (which is presumed to be the true form of the forest demon), and after some dialogue, it "opens" and allows Mario access into a more tranquil forest before he enters a tube, instructions given to him as the world around him distorts. At the end, a voice mentions "I'll miss you." before a Smash to Black. To signify that this is one of the game's definitive endings, this is one of the only ones to have closing credits.

  • "Disorder" ending: Backtracking in the penultimate room of the final ending allows Peach to enter the room behind her, where Mario is shaking on a bed. He then suddenly disappears as the TV nearby turns on, with Mario's head twitching on it and a voice gleefully claiming that they're free. As they monologue, the head and Peach vanish as the scene freezes. Cue end credits.
  • "Lighter" ending: Backtracking in the last room with Mario or Peach and having the lighter equipped will have them light a fire, presumably to burn the forest to the ground. The cat shows up, asking if this is how they thought it would turn out, and affirming that they know what comes next. After the dialogue ends, the wall behind the cat, the cat itself and the fire all vanish, allowing your character to leave the forest. Uniquely, after the end credits of this ending, one of two situations occur:
    • If playing as Mario, there's a Flashback with Mario and Peach in a house together... and the alien lifeform hiding in a room behind the fireplace.
    • If playing as Peach, there's instead a stinger set at night, depicting Peach looking out at the horizon before the cat calls for her and leads her back the way she came, though she hesitates to step back on the bridge again. Cue a Smash to Black and Title Drop.
