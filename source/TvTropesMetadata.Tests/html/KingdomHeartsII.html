Video Game / Kingdom Hearts II

Kingdom Hearts II is an Action RPG published and developed by Square Enix in collaboration with Disney, and the third game in their Kingdom Hearts series, first released for the PlayStation 2 on December 22, 2005 in Japan, March 28, 2006 in America, and September 28/29 in Europe and Australia.

One year after they were victorious over the Heartless threat, Sora, Donald and Goofy wake up from a long slumber and find themselves in the quaint village of Twilight Town, not knowing what happened to bring them there. Suddenly, King Mickey appears to guide them to the wise Yen Sid, who explains to them the new crisis impacting the worlds.

During the year that the trio were asleep, a new breed of enemy has emerged from the shadows: the Nobodies, the reanimated husks of the people whose hearts were stolen by The Heartless. Most powerful and intelligent among them are a syndicate known as Organization XIII, who aim to unleash the Heartless upon the worlds once more and use them to collect more hearts for their plan, uncaring of the ensuing collateral damage. It's now up to Sora, Keyblade in hand and friends by his side, to battle the Heartless and Nobodies alike to save the worlds from chaos once again.

But as Sora resumes his travels, multiple questions plague him: why was he asleep for so long, and who was the one to make sure he woke up? Who or what brought him to Twilight Town? What happened to Riku and Kairi while he was asleep? Who are Organization XIII, and what do they truly want? And just what is Sora's connection to Roxas, a blond-haired kid from Twilight Town, and one whose cozy life fell apart right as his was being rebuilt?

The trio revisits a few expanded/remixed worlds from the first game (based on Winnie-the-Pooh, Hercules, The Little Mermaid, Aladdin (loosely adapting the sequel, The Return of Jafar), and The Nightmare Before Christmas), as well as all new ones (based on Mulan, Beauty and the Beast, Classic Disney Shorts (most prominently Steamboat Willie), The Lion King, and even the live-action films Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl and TRON).

In terms of gameplay, Kingdom Hearts II returns to a style more reminiscent of the first game after Chain of Memories made a number of alterations to be better suited for handhelds, though there have been significant changes. The platforming and exploration elements have been done away with in lieu of a more combat-focused game, with Sora's arsenal being much more diverse. A variety of combo modifiers, Combination Attacks with allies called Limits, and the ability to power up with Drive Forms provide many new ways to approach battles. Optional quick-time events called Reaction Commands also allow Sora to use special techniques against his enemies, making for even more flashy combat and allowing for cinematic moments during boss encounters.

Like many Square games, Kingdom Hearts II's was re-released in Japan with English voices plus extra content as Kingdom Hearts II Final Mix (included in a bundle called Kingdom Hearts II Final Mix+ alongside an Video Game Remake of Chain of Memories titled Re:Chain of Memories). Later, in December 2014, Square Enix released Kingdom Hearts HD II.5 ReMIX for the PlayStation 3, another bundle containing HD versions of both Kingdom Hearts II Final Mix and Birth By Sleep Final Mix along with the cutscenes of Re:coded and Trophy support. This same bundle was later re-released alongside the preceding HD I.5 ReMIX compilation (containing HD versions of Kingdom Hearts I Final Mix and Re:Chain of Memories along with the cutscenes of 358/2 Days) on a single disc for the PlayStation 4 initially and later the Xbox One with 60fps support. That compilation, alongside Kingdom Hearts HD II.8 Final Chapter Prologue, Kingdom Hearts III + Re Mind, and Kingdom Hearts: Melody of Memory, was released for Windows PC via the Epic Games Store on March 30, 2021 and on Steam on June 13, 2024.

Kingdom Hearts II has its own manga adaptation, which at certain points goes even Darker and Edgier than the game, as well as a novelization.

The game is followed by Kingdom Hearts coded in production and chronological order, though said game only released in Japan prior to its remake for the Nintendo DS. The next game in the series to release internationally would be Kingdom Hearts 358/2 Days, a prequel which explores Roxas and his backstory.
