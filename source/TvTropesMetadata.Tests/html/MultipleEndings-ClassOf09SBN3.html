Warning: Spoilers are unmarked on these pages. You Have Been Warned.

Class of '09 is a multiple choice game where the player can get 5 different endings in Flip-Side, 7 in Re-Up and 15 in the original game based on the choices they make, along with text messages afterwards as epilogues, some of which can be rather... unexpected.
open/close all folders 

Class of '09 
  • Nicole and Jecka agree with photography teacher Mr White's white nationalism teachings. They inadvertently aid him in building a chlorine bomb that he detonates at a Puerto Rican civil rights rally, where he is killed and the two are arrested. Nicole ends by mentioning while she was initially not racist, she was forced to so she could join a white supremacy prison gang for protection. She receives a text from Jeffrey quoting the Zodiac killer.
  • After Jeffrey joins Mr. White's white pride party, Nicole convinces him to leave after offering to take him on a date. Jecka and other members kill Jeffrey for leaving the group. The FBI investigates, and since over 95% of the school were involved in the club, the school is effectively closed and nearly every teacher and student is arrested. Jecka becomes a white supremacist in prison, and Nicole ends by mentioning that apathy leads to "stupid shit". She receives a text from Jeffrey wondering if he was the only one that joined the party for friendship, and that they were the only ones that "even pretended to like him"
  • Nicole films members of the school white pride party chanting in the school and uploads the video on a Nation of Islam web forum. It gains notoriety and the school is burned down in an attack, killing everyone inside it. Nicole ends by mentioning she called in sick that day and that it, "sucks to be in the white pride party". She receives a text from Jeffrey wondering if it can be classified as a hate crime if "everyone had to go".
  • After telling everyone at school about Jeffrey's sexual fetishes and being unsympathetic when Jeffrey confides in her that he is going to kill himself, Jeffrey commits a school shooting, killing Nicole and many of her classmates. She ends by mentioning people will remember Jeffrey as an evil mastermind opposed to a pathetic pervert, and her as a kind, thoughtful girl opposed to a mean spirited bully that caused the entire incident. She receives a text from Jeffrey ranting about how unfair the school has been to him, referencing a girl complaining about "only having her boyfriend to talk to", and how he desires to destroy the world she lives in.

  • Nicole agrees to date her gym teacher, Coach Colby, in exchange of money. After agreeing to go to his place, he strangles Nicole to death and then has sex with her dead body. She ends by mentioning that Jecka was cool and that she will see her father soon, but that her mother and brother can "fuck off and burn in hell" with her. She receives a text from Jeffrey saying that Nicole is being memorialized at the school, and ponders if he would receive such honor if he were to die.
  • After Kylar jumps off a roof and kills himself for Nicole, she starts a YouTube vlog about it. Her video becomes viral and she becomes a YouTube star speaking at assemblies and taking paying sponsorships. She ends by mentioning she tried to hang out with the guy who plays McLovin, but he ignored her. She receives a text from Jeffrey admitting his envy of Nicole for her fame, how much he hates her, yet ends by mentioning he can still be her friend.
  • After Kylar's suicide, the male students become scared of Nicole, believing she can convince people to kill themselves. Nicole coerces Jeffrey into doing her homework and slipping her exam answers until graduation. Nicole becomes valedictorian as a result. In her speech, she says that she just used quotes from an AA brochure, and gives a, "special thanks to lacrosse". She receives a text from Jeffrey where he complains about how women "want men to show vulnerability" yet it doesn't work out for him.
  • Nicole breaks into Kylar's house after his death and steals his large stash of Percocet. She nearly overdoses on them a week later and is involuntarily sent to rehab. She ends by mentioning meth heads with no teeth constantly hit on her, and makes a half joke, half cry for help remark about hanging herself. She receives a text from an unknown number, possibly Kylar, posing as a spiritual message, with it actually reading "kill and fuck a fat bitch".
  • Nicole claims to her mother that Crispin sexually harassed her in an attempt to get a new wardrobe, but she attempts to have him expelled instead. She cannot afford the legal fees and her and Nicole are forced to move again. Nicole ends by saying she isn't surprised since her mother's six monthly alimony checks go to her dyeing her hair. She receives a text from Jeffrey saying it's a "non news factor" and "at least people talk to you".

  • After Jeffrey takes a photo of Nicole in her underwear, Nicole breaks into the school's photography lab and destroys it to prevent the photo from being developed. She is caught by the police in the act, is given a state-wide expulsion, and is forced to work at a fast food restaurant as her mother refuses to move again. She ends by mentioning that her mother has become physically abusive with her for "becoming a disappointment". She receives a text from Jeffrey where he fantasizes about killing women for ignoring him for so long.
  • After successfully making a false sexual assault claim against Mr. White, Nicole is awarded $500,000 in a lawsuit, drops out of high school, cuts ties with her family and moves to LA. She ends by mentioning she picked up an expensive drug habit and has met another girl who is also named Nicole. She receives a text from Jeffrey complaining that when he is harassed, he is told to get over it, while Nicole is awarded.
  • When pressured by a lawyer in court over her false sexual assault claim against Mr. White, Nicole admits it was fraudulent. Her family becomes financially ruined by the legal fees and lose their house. Her mother remarries and kicks Nicole to the streets, where she becomes homeless living in a cardboard box. She ends by mentioning that living life being honest makes it much more difficult. She receives a snarky text from Jeffrey offering to let Nicole stay at his place.
  • Nicole gets through the school year without many issues by using and manipulating others for her own benefit, and during her graduation ceremony, she verbally outs the school faculty for their predatory tendencies, which gets Principal Lynn fired. Nicole then learns that Lynn was just like her in her younger years and slept her way to her position as principal, but unlike her, Lynn made the mistake of thinking that she owed the people she used, which resulted in the school's staff all having blackmail material against her and her letting their crimes go unpunished. Nicole ends by pondering on her tendency to manipulate those around her, and contemplates a career in politics. She receives a text from Jeffrey explaining that he works at a gas station now, as his lack of extracurricular activities caused him to be rejected by all colleges, and that he just saw someone who laughed at him in school arrive in a fancy new car.

  • Nicole is unable to cope with maintaining her "hostage friendships" with several boys and attempts suicide by slitting her wrists. Her mother saves her life mid attempt and she is committed into a psych ward until her 18th birthday. She ends by mentioning that the feeling of true happiness is always weaker than the desire to kill oneself. She receives a text from Jeffrey saying, "Fuck you. I have to hear about you and you aren't even dead".
  • Perhaps the most brutal ending, if Nicole is "nice" to everyone she meets, she becomes overwhelmed and legitimately depressed trying to maintain her friendships with various men, and this causes Jecka to stop talking to her because she feels like Nicole is prioritizing her friendships with the boys over her. The boys then all get angry over a false rumor of her having sex with the entire football team, which was started by Coach Colby because Nicole rejected his advances, and they begin to insult, harass and bully her on a daily basis over it. She seeks genuine help from either the guidance counselor or her mother, but both are unsympathetic to her problems, with her mother outright threatening to kick her out of the house if she doesn't return to school. Distraught, Nicole leaves a note mentioning that being kind and nonjudgmental to men like her mother wanted her to "doesn't work when they all want to have sex with you", before hanging herself. She receives a text from Jecka simply saying, "hey. miss u. I'm sorry".

Re-Up 
  • Nicole decides to ruin Megan's life by seducing her boyfriend Hunter simply because Megan was rude and bossy to her as a student teacher in theater class. To achieve this, she manipulates Hunter into sending her nude pictures of him, which she then sends to Megan in the middle of a school play she had organized, causing Megan to hysterically berate Hunter and have a panic attack in front of the audience. She ends by mentioning that people will do crazy things when pushed too far. She receives a text from Hunter telling her that Megan broke up with him, and asks her if she wants a serious relationship.
  • Nicole gets expelled from school and her mother subsequently kicks her out of the house for it, becoming homeless and having to be an online escort trading sexual favors to survive and buy drugs. She only gets out of it by blackmailing and then reporting Mr. Lorre, her former Art teacher who got her expelled in the first place, for attempting to solicit her, leading to her expulsion being rescinded and her mother allowing her to move back in. She ends by mentioning street rules don't exist "on the actual street" and how people will do anything, no matter how depraved, just to survive. She receives a text from a man asking for a whipped cream handjob while she pretends to be his daughter.
  • Nicole starts selling crack as part of a fake community service charity for her civics class project. Principal Lynn becomes suspicious and calls the police on Nicole, leading to Nicole being arrested and sent to prison for three years. While imprisoned, Nicole begins self-reflecting on her actions, only to immediately backtrack by vowing to torture and kill Lynn for ratting her out once she leaves jail. She receives a text from her brother saying he sold all of her possessions and that she doesn't have a place at their house anymore.
  • After accepting Ari's confession, Nicole enters a relationship with her, though she becomes verbally, emotionally and mentally abusive towards Ari under the belief that Ari will simply walk away from the relationship if she is feeling uncomfortable. Ari eventually breaks up with her, but decides to start dating Hunter because, as much as she likes girls and feels nothing towards boys, being with Nicole has completely changed her perspective on pursuing relationships with other women, causing her to associate it with trauma. Nicole ponders the alternative of being abused or being alone, how bisexual girls feel around men, and ends wondering if she somehow learned something from all this. She receives a drunk text from Ari saying she doesn't miss Nicole but just the sex, and offers to be friends with benefits.

  • After rejecting Ari's confession, Nicole is painted as a homophobe by her classmates. After Ari's house is burnt down by the Coach Colby's "Straight Club", Nicole reveals she had to date Ari to ensure she won't be blamed for the whole thing, becoming trapped in a "hostage relationship". She ends by mentioning how annoying Ari is and hopes she moves away after graduation. She receives a text telling her that police officers from her 911 call are 1 hour away and to simply hide until they get there.
  • Nicole gets roped into a suicide pact by Emily, the latter dying of an overdose in their remedial English class while Nicole just barely avoids the same fate due to diluting her share of the pills she was meant to use. She mentions that Emily is far more evil and messed up than her, but at least she "has the serotonin to not kill herself." She receives a goodbye text from Emily, who confesses she was attracted to Nicole and hopes their bodies can collapse together.
  • Nicole gets Ms. Ames, her remedial English teacher, fired by intentionally writing an essay quoting Mein Kampf and having Ms. Ames read it out loud during a PTA assembly, which was got her reported to the ADL by a Jewish deli worker acting as a caterer for the event. Ms. Ames' racist past is then unearthed during the whole scandal, which gets her teaching license revoked, much to Nicole's satisfaction. She receives a promotional text from the Jewish worker's deli offering a catering event that excludes schools and other antisemitic activities.

Flip-Side 
  • Jecka has developed an alcohol addiction due to the stress of her home life with her Abusive Dad, her studies, and her new job at Hot Topic, and she and Nicole are forced to bail out from a party after the police comes to raid the place, forcing Jecka to get behind the wheel while drunk. She ends up getting Ari killed in a traffic accident as the latter was making deliveries for Domino's Pizza. Jecka takes no responsibility for Ari's death, blaming her father and her new job for her alcohol addiction, which gets her acquitted from all charges in court thanks to her crying. She receives a text from Ari's manager, who offers her a job as a pizza delivery girl and asks her if she has any nude pictures of Ari.
  • While working at FYE, Jecka and Kelly stumble upon a warehouse full of illegal pornography. The counselor then appears and shoots and kills Kelly and tries to kill Jecka to keep her quiet about it, but he himself is gunned down by the FBI... who reveal that they themselves also consume the pornography, and they sell Jecka to a Taliban sex trafficking ring to ensure that she never tells anyone. Jecka mentions that she has not seen the daylight in two years, and finishes by saying that she hopes Nicole doesn't get a job at FYE so that she doesn't meet the same fate... before backtracking and saying that maybe it would be a good thing so she could have a friend with her. She receives a text from George W. Bush telling her that Barack Obama has sent a presidential pardon message to the Taliban in an attempt to have her released and returned to the United States, and finishes off by saying that "Kanye was right".
  • Jecka cuts Nicole out of her foot fetish business. Not long after, she starts to be endlessly harassed by her many clients. She then goes home to find Nicole engaging in foot fetish roleplay with her father. Distraught, she commits suicide by overdosing on prescription pills, mentioning that people can laugh at her for being a sex worker all they want, but that she will at least be "gorgeous forever". She receives a text from a 34-year old Basement Dweller pedophile who explicitly tells her about his many fetishes and how he wants a relationship with her.

  • Jecka lets Nicole participate in her foot fetish business, and while taking Jeffrey as a client, Nicole tricks him into overdosing on a large amount of prescription drugs and alcohol. Jecka tries to call 911 in hopes of saving Jeffrey, as he is her best client, but Nicole, knowing that Jeffrey will tell the police about what she did if he is is resuscitated, blackmails Jecka by saying that if they find her guilty for attempted murder, she will drag Jecka along with her, as she specifically gave Jeffrey Jecka's pills, causing Jecka to not go through with the call, letting Jeffrey die of an overdose. She ends by mentioning that she has decided to quit the foot fetish business, saying that she too would have ended up dead if she kept up with it, and ends by mentioning that she's glad that Jeffrey is dead. She receives a text from Jeffrey's mother, who admits that she had long given up on Jeffrey and that she is relieved that he is gone forever.
  • Going back to the suicide route of the first game, we see Jecka's perspective of what happened after she stopped talking to Nicole due to the latter prioritizing her "hostage friendships" with various boys over her. As it turns out, Jecka lashed out at Nicole because she herself was dealing with several problems of her own, namely her father's abuse at home and having to date her AP History teacher, Mr. Katz, in exchange of him bumping her grades up. Ms. Ames eventually calls the police on Mr. Katz, and after Jecka's father becomes aware of this he threatens to beat Jecka, leading her to run to Nicole's house to hide. She finds the house seemingly empty, and goes to Nicole's bedroom to discover her hanging dead body along with her suicide note, realizing that Nicole wasn't ignoring her on purpose and merely got roped in with all those boys due to her mother's horrible advice. She ends by telling Ms. Ames that there is no one that really cares about her or all the trauma she has experienced. She receives a text from Nicole simply saying "why cant u help me".
