This page summarizes the many, potential endings of The Hundred Line -Last Defense Academy-. As a page about endings, all spoilers will be unmarked.

open/close all folders 

First Playthrough

Route 0 
  • Ending 0: It All Starts Now: Go through the motions of everything that happens without player input. The player is guaranteed to see this ending, which is not considered one of the 100 endings advertised, on their first playthrough. Eito suggests going along with the invaders' demands to get home. Nozomi begs the group that they need to stay and fight, only for them to turn against her with the sudden reveal that they know of her secret, creating a rift between her and Takumi in the process, Nozomi believing he had spread it against her wishes. On the day of the agreement, the negotiations turn out to be a trap by the Supreme Commander, who kills Nozomi when she runs to assist everyone stuck in the "escape pod" provided. Takumi kills the Supreme Commander and absorbs her cryptoglobin, giving him the power to finally understand the boy made of Undying Flames, who is revealed to be the power source of Last Defense Academy, the source of the Flames, and the battery for the Doomsday Device behind the locked doors. Eito then shows his true colors and misanthropy by destroying the generator, leading to a fight to the death and Takumi absorbing his cryptoglobin as the entire academy shuts down. Before he merges with Takumi entirely by proxy, the Ghost Boy shows himself one last time to tell Takumi that the merging of him, Eito, and Takumi's hemoanima has enhanced his Specialist ability to the point that he would be able to return to the first day of his time at Last Defense Academy. On the Hundredth Day, it's revealed the Academy had an escape pod prepped for the group all along, and Takumi decides not to board so that he can go back in time and Set Right What Once Went Wrong. Shortly after, the escape pod returns to Earth with nothing but a lone walkie-talkie, with Tsubasa and Takemaru trying to warn Takumi that something horrible and wrong is happening up in the Artificial Satellite.

Spare Eito Routes

Last Defense Academy 2nd Scenario Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Let Hiruko attempt to destroy Nigou, Save Gaku, Let the Undying Flames Boy join the team, Spare Eito again, Let Shouma kill Eva, Keep fighting for humanity, Allow Eito to rejoin the team, Defy Sirei one last time

  • Ending 001: Farewell, Last Defense Academy: The "true" ending to the extent there is one, as due to the laws of Time Travel in this setting all endings happen in The Multiverse (Word of God explicitly describes its name as a Pun; rather than being a Golden Ending, it is an ending where you learn a lot of the truth). The students discover most of their lives are faked, they are actually Super-Soldier Futurans grown to serve as humanity's champions with the exception of Nozomi, but decide to side with them because of Nozomi's kindness and realizing humanity is out of options, seeking to at least save Futurans in genetic vaults once they have their new home. V'ehxness, having cannibalized most of her fellow Commanders, learns to manipulate the Undying Flames and creates a missile she uses to destroy the Artificial Satellite, leaving Nozomi the sole human alive — but is killed before she can take over the planet as the "new God". Sirei, never having been programmed with instructions on what to do if humanity is destroyed, proceeds to activate the Doomsday Device and the group is forced to Mercy Kill Shion to stop the destruction of the Futurans, killing everyone except Nozomi. She finds a party of Futuran children who she makes peace with, implying that humanity's memory at least will live on.

Reset Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Let Hiruko attempt to destroy Nigou, Save Gaku, Let the Undying Flames Boy join the team, Spare Eito again, Let Shouma kill Eva, Keep fighting for humanity, Allow Eito to rejoin the team, Don’t defy Sirei at the end

  • Ending 002: Welcome to Last Defense Academy: This ending follows the "Last Defense Academy 2nd Scenario" Route until the 100th day where Sirei orders the students to evacuate the Academy so they won't get killed by the omni-directional attack. Instead of rebelling against him, Takumi and the students obey his last order. Sirei reconfigures the escape pod to return to the planet after all Futurans are killed so that the students can repopulate the planet. After the Futurans are wiped out by the Undying Flames, the students' memories of their adventure are removed on their return to the Academy, their backstories are replaced with happier alternatives, and Sirei instructs them to rebuild civilization as their new mission for humanity's future.

Goodbye Eito Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Let Hiruko attempt to destroy Nigou, Save Gaku, Let the Undying Flames Boy join the team, Spare Eito again, Let Shouma kill Eva, Keep fighting for humanity, Don’t allow Eito to rejoin the team

  • Ending 003: Praying For Your Happiness: Instead of accepting Eito back into the team after he blinded himself, Takumi rejects bringing him back, and he leaves the Academy to seek refuge in the Second-to-Last Defense Academy while grappling with guilt and possible forgiveness. When he arrives there, he discovers that Sirei packaged an Undying Flame Bomb that he was told to use if V'ehxness came for him, though he speculates if he was actually supposed to use it to kill himself. Soon, he meets a group of Futuran children refugees who decided to take refuge in the Academy, and he quickly relates to their struggles of not being forgiven as they disobeyed orders not to go outside and strayed away from their own group. Unfortunately, the Invaders catch on to their hiding spot and attack the Academy. Eito tells the children to run and that forgiveness is based on actions after mistakes instead of feelings of guilt, and as they flee he transforms and fights the Invaders in a Last Stand. When he's inevitably overwhelmed, he detonates the bomb, reveals that he asked Sirei to implant Fake Memories of him bonding and having fun with the other students and prays for the others happiness and victory in his final moments. The ultimate fate of the SDU is unrevealed.

Rebellion Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Let Hiruko attempt to destroy Nigou, Save Gaku, Let the Undying Flames Boy join the team, Spare Eito again, Let Shouma kill Eva, Abandon humanity, Don’t join the rebellion (Ending 004), Join the rebellion, Save Nozomi (Ending 005), Save Kamyuhn (Ending 006)

The Special Defense Unit decides to rebel against Sirei and the Artificial Satellite, and joins arms with the Futuran Resistance who wish to defeat both V'ehxness and stop humanity's invasion.

  • Ending 004: Failed Alliance: Refusing to join Kamyuhn's rebellion makes her kill Nozomi as Eito agrees that she seems suspicious, with Takumi killing her in retaliation. Futurum is consumed by a deadly war between the Futurans and humanity, and Takumi spends years fighting both armies on the now irradiated planet, separated from his fellow classmates.
  • Ending 005: My War: After V'ehxness self-destructs, Nozomi and Kamyuhn are mortally wounded by debris and request that their cryptoglobin be used to heal the other. Takumi decides to save Nozomi instead of Kamyuhn, and war breaks out between the Futurans and humanity. Ending the conflict isn't near or easy since Kamyuhn, the one person capable of uniting the Futurans, is dead, but Nozomi won't give up striving for peace as she teaches at a school she and Takumi built.
  • Ending 006: Seeds of Hope: Takumi chooses to save Kamyuhn, and while this leads to a war between humanity and the Futurans, Takumi and Kamyuhn manage to rebuild and unite the Futuran society, and eventually negotiate peace with Humans and end the war 15 years later. Although they have succeeded in creating peace on Futurum, Takumi still feels guilty over Nozomi's death and can only hope that she's happy with the result.

Eva Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Let Hiruko attempt to destroy Nigou, Save Gaku, Let the Undying Flames Boy join the team, Spare Eito again, Do not let Shouma kill Eva (Protect Eva), Save Last Defense Academy (Ending 007), Stay here (Ending 008)

Takumi refuses to let Shouma absorb Eva's hemoanima, considering her their friend. However this leads to Eva managing to temporarily escape her brainwashing and kill Shouma in self defense. The rest of the team loses respect for Takumi for allowing Shouma to die, and he tries to ease his guilt by talking to the brainwashed Eva about her family. This causes Eva to finally break free of her brainwashing, and Takumi deserts the SDU to help her escape and find her family, and the two of them start growing feelings for each other in the process.

  • Ending 007: Thank You: Takumi chooses to protect Last Defense Academy, with Eva insisting on joining them. After an intense battle, Eva slays V’ehxness, before dying in Takumi’s arms. With V’ehxness dead and the Undying Flames exhausted destroying Dahl’xia, humanity and the Futurans open negotiations, but Takumi has lost the person he fell in love with.
  • Ending 008: The Wanderers: Takumi decides to stay with Eva, leaving Last Defense Academy to be destroyed. Years later, after the end of the humans, V’ehxness has been suppressing the rebels. Takumi and Eva (now pregnant) haven’t seen Kamyuhn in years, but are content to be with each other.

Multiple Eitos Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Let Hiruko attempt to destroy Nigou, Save Gaku, Let the Undying Flames Boy join the team, Kill Eito, Say 11th Eito isn’t here, Say “I Lose” (Ending 009), Say “You Win” (Ending 010), Say you found the 11th Eito, Embrace the hate (Ending 011), Reject the hate (Ending 012)

Takumi refuses to give Eito a second chance and immediately kills him while absorbing his cryptoglobin. The next day, Takumi suddenly starts seeing everyone as Eito with his appearance and voice, with Eito's consciousness explaining that due to Takumi absorbing his body twice, Eito's cognitive defect has now affected him as well. Amused by Takumi's situation, Eito gives him a challenge; he has created a fake Eito within the academy and gives Takumi a few days to find out which one is the fake or else he will take over his body.

  • Ending 009: Endurance Match: Takumi kills himself repeatedly to drive out Eito’s essence. Unfortunately, Eito ends up in control of the body.
  • Ending 010: Together Forever: Takumi gives up the fight against Eito. Eito, disappointed by his surrender, fails to take control of Takumi’s body, but does turn his reflection into another clone of Eito.
  • Ending 011: Eternal Hate: Takumi succumbs to his hatred of Eito and massacres everyone, ending with Sirei and the fire baby. Eito takes joy at how alike they have become.
  • Ending 012: A Tragic End: Takumi refuses to fall to Eito’s manipulation, and convinces his remaining friends to kill him.

Serial Battles Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Let Hiruko attempt to destroy Nigou, Save Gaku, Don’t let the Undying Flames Boy join the team, Say “My fight is over” (Ending 013), Say “My fight isn’t over yet” (Ending 014)

Takumi decides against letting Shion join the team, and during the next battle with Eito, Shion steps in to use his flames to defeat him. Thanks to the Undying Flames weakening at the worst possible time, V'ehxness discovers how to summon alternate-universe versions of the Commanders using a device she found in the Last Defense Academy, sending them in a nearly constant wave, culminating in an attempted tag-team with another version of herself. Defeating her banishes all the alternates, leaving the exhausted SDU to finally rest.

  • Ending 013: The War is Over: After the 100 days are up, Takumi accepts the fight is over and boards the escape pod with the others.
  • Ending 014: One More Time: Takumi resolves to go back in time again, to ensure there’s an ending where everyone survives.

Conspiracy Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Let Hiruko attempt to destroy Nigou, Save Kurara, Don’t take the red pill (Ending 015), Take the red pill, Don’t help the Anti-Exodus Faction (Ending 016), Help the Anti-Exodus Faction, Persuade Hiruko (Ending 017), Persuade Kurara, Say “No” to “Were our friends’ deaths in vain?” (Ending 018), Say “...” to “Were our friends’ deaths in vain?”, Try not to destroy Sirei (Ending 019), Plan to destroy Sirei, Hesitate to destroy Sirei (Ending 020), Destroy Sirei, Reply “...” for “Everything we did…” (Ending 021), Reply “Was pointless” for “Everything we did…” (Ending 022)

Nigou suffers a blow to the head, which causes him to malfunction and allows a hidden computer virus inside of him to take hold. The virus tells Takumi that his name is Gotoh, and he was planted in Nigou by a rebel faction of humans inside the Artificial Satellite.

  • Ending 015: A Fair Point: Takumi refuses to take Gotoh’s pill. In response, Gotoh promptly kills him.
  • Ending 016: Just A Test?: Takumi refuses to join the Anti-Exodus faction. In response, Gotoh claims the whole conversation was a test, then promptly kills him.
  • Ending 017: Plans Exposed: Takumi tries to persuade Hiruko first. She instead informs Sirei of the conspiracy, who promptly captures Takumi.
  • Ending 018: Empty Words: Takumi and Kurara decide to keep fighting, despite The Conspiracy’s intel. In response, Gotoh promptly kills them both.
  • Ending 019: Out of Ideas: Takumi and Kurara decide against destroying Sirei, wanting to find a different solution. Unfortunately, they fail to think of a better option, giving time for Sirei to catch on to their plot and attack them.
  • Ending 020: Faltering Resolve: Takumi hesitates to attack Sirei during Gotoh’s ambush, getting spotted in the process. Sirei promptly attacks the two.
  • Ending 021: Liar: Nozomi convinces Takumi that they should keep fighting, not wanting to believe all they had done was pointless. Unfortunately, Gotoh will not accept any such resolve, and promptly activates the bombs implanted in them.
  • Ending 022: Lambs to the Slaughter: Takumi convinces the rest of the group to give up the fight and flee to the Artificial Satellite. However, before departing, Gotoh reveals to Takumi that the intel he provided about the team’s families was fabricated to make them give up fighting, though he insists the rest of what he revealed was true. Too tired to fight anymore, Takumi quietly accepts this. The team silently leaves the Academy, facing an uncertain future.

Casual Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Take Eva prisoner, Stop Hiruko from destroying Nigou, Return to reality (Ending 023), Stay in the dream (Ending 024)

Takumi steps in to stop Hiruko from destroying Nigou, only to suddenly end up getting knocked unconscious by Sirei upon realizing his memories have been tampered with. Takumi later wakes up with no recollection of what happened, and then spends some quality time with his teammates without much worry, while at the same time having a feeling that something is off.

  • Ending 023: Face Reality: Sirei traps Takumi’s mind in an illusion after he starts questioning his loyalty to the commander. At the end, he chooses to escape the illusion and go down fighting the invaders.
  • Ending 024: Embrace Fantasy: In the end, Takumi chooses to remain in the dream world as his body dies against V’ehxness. In the dream, everyone is sitting around the classroom drinking milk cartons and having the same problem Takumi did: not being able to get the last drop of milk out.

Box of Blessings Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Kill Eva, Look for Kako, Join Exploration Team, Join Girls Team, Open odd-numbered boxes (Ending 026), Open prime-numbered boxes (Ending 025), Join Boys Team, Handle him myself (Ending 028), Let Gaku do it (Ending 027), Join Defense Team, Join Shouma’s Team, Tell Shouma “You can run if you want” (Ending 030), Tell Shouma to face his true feelings (Ending 029), Join Nozomi’s Team, Seal the boxes (Ending 031), Burn the boxes (Ending 032)

The SDU learns of the existence of powerful native artifacts called "Boxes of Blessings", and that they will grant a wish if they are opened. However they must all be collected and opened in certain order because there are identical looking "Boxes of Calamity" hidden among them.

  • Ending 025: Blessed With a Miracle: After defeating V'ehxness in the final battle and collecting all the Boxes of Blessings, now all that's left is to choose which ones to open. Takumi chooses to open the prime-numbered boxes, and his wish of everyone living to see day 100 comes true as Darumi and Kyoshika are restored back to life. The issue with Eito and the fate of Futurum stays unresolved, but Takumi doesn't care because he believes that the power of everyone's friendship will conquer all future obstacles in their path.
  • Ending 026: Cursed with Calamity: Takumi chooses to open the odd numbered boxes and opens a Box of Calamity by mistake. Everyone goes mad and kills each other while raving about killing in the name of the planet. Eito also makes his appearance and kills Takumi while mocking his poor decision.
  • Ending 027: Gaku’s Harem: After V'ehxness's defeat Eito suddenly appears and immobilizes the whole team and begins opening the boxes himself. Gaku manages to get up and Takumi chooses to trust in his plan. As Eito opens the boxes and starts saying his wish, Gaku tackles him, and in a clear reference to Classic Dragon Ball hijacks Eito's wish by yelling "Give me the life of a harem romcom protag!" Darumi and Kyoshika are restored back to life, and immediately begin swooning over Gaku. Takumi and the rest of the team are utterly flabbergasted, but accept the situation since Gaku still saved everyone and all their friends will live to see day 100.
  • Ending 028: The Value of Trust: Takumi doesn't trust Gaku and tackles him down because he knows his plan would be something reckless or impulsive. He sprints towards Eito but Eito immobilizes him easily, and decides to take his leave and open the boxes on his own leisure. Takumi is left lying down with bitter regret over not trusting Gaku, concluding that the reckless and impulsive one was him all along.

Box of Calamity Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Kyoshika, Kill Eva, Look for the Shadowy Figure, Attack the Shadowy Figure (Ending 033), Turn to face the voice, Go help Sirei, Take Gaku’s side (Ending 036), Take Hiruko’s side, Do not reveal the culprit (Ending 035), Reveal the culprit (Ending 034), Pick up the box, Don’t tell everyone what you saw, Don’t resist Gaku (Ending 038), Resist Gaku (Ending 037), Tell everyone what you saw, Eva was not an evil spirit (Ending 039), Eva was an evil spirit (Ending 040)

This route has mostly the same premise as the "Box of Blessings" route, but deals more with the consequences resulting from the Boxes of Calamity.

Takumi tries to attack the shadow, but is slashed by the scythe, then forced to watch them kill Nozomi. Takumi wakes up some unknown time later and finds everyone is gone, until he comes across a dying Hiruko. She tells him it's now Day 100 as he went into a coma after Nozomi's death, the war is also now lost with everyone dead. Upon hearing footsteps behind him, the killer is revealed to be Kako, bringing the scythe down on Takumi.

Just as she's about to convince the group to let her on the pod, Tsubasa sets off her bomb, killing her, leaving only her legs behind. Takumi finishes off the Queen before the group gets into the pod. The war is over, but Takumi asks what the point was in traveling back in time if it led to worse deaths. Hiruko tells him that she'll remember, and another version of her in another timeline will too.

Nozomi thanks him for not ratting her out as the G'ie Queen, while Takumi concedes that even an alien parasite kills because it wants to live and survive, just like everyone else has done this whole time. They both share a short moment together, and the G'ie Queen kills him seconds before they both die to Undying Flames.

Cult of Takumi Route 

Ending Path (click to expand) Spare Eito, Reveal your time-travel secret, Save Second-Last Defense Academy, Reveal Nozomi’s secret, Sacrifice Takemaru, Believe Nozomi (Ending 042), Believe Eito (Ending 041)

After sacrificing Takemaru, Takumi ingests some pills made by Yugamu that boosts his charisma and pheromones to be a better team leader and to improve his self-esteem. This results in all the other students falling madly in love with him, setting up a "reward" system as an excuse to be uncomfortably intimate with him and eventually setting up a cult centered around him. Nozomi, who isn't affected as much due to her weak hemoanima, questions if the current state of affairs will really be for the better and asks Takumi if he wants to be cured of his new condition, but Eito retorts that Takumi's newfound charisma is needed to unify the students under him and that they want him to be their ruler.

  • Ending 041: Overlord: Agreeing with Eito will make the other students brainwash Nozomi to love Takumi as well, and Takumi quickly rationalizes his newfound authority as necessary. V'ehxness arrives early on Day 54, where she declares her love for Takumi and desire to take him for herself instead of ruling over Futurum and winning the war. After killing V'ehxness, and Nozomi's "reward" time where he rapes her, Takumi fully embraces his role as the students' ruler and aims to take over both humanity and the Futurans with his brainwashed harem cult at his side.
  • Ending 042: Living Idol: Instead of agreeing with Eito, Takumi agrees with Nozomi to be cured of his condition and give everyone's free will back. The next day, Yugamu tricks Takumi into swallowing pills that are supposedly the cure, but they actually completely paralyze his body, leaving him unable to do anything but breathe and blink. The students declare their intention to keep him alive and immobile as they turn him into their "god", and beat Nozomi to death for trying to "take away their god". Soon after, Eito rapes Takumi while professing his undying love for him, and Takumi mentally shuts down, resigning himself to his fate as a living symbol.

V'ehxness Route

Reunited, they resolve to face the future together.
