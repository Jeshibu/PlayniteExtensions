Multiple Endings / #0 IGGY'S CASTLE

Following
Go To
Edit PageRelatedHistoryAdd ReviewDiscussionTo DoPage Source
VideoGameNightmareFuelTriviaYMMVMultipleEnding…Create Subpage

The initial October 2023 release of #0 IGGY'S CASTLE had 5 endings. A later patch added two more endings (Endings 4 & 6). Then a third patch released in March 2024 added an additional 6 endings for a total of 13 endings that exist in 2.0. A fourth patch added 12 new endings, giving the game 25 endings total as of 3.0. note The ending order and names are taken directly from Wakana's video series on reaching each ending. The ending text will be added as notes for clarity's sake. Take warning that these notes will not be spoiler tagged.

  • Ending 1 - The Genocidenote BAD ENDING - You crushed Toad Town. - Buy the flashlight with Mario's soul, defeat the Face Guardian, and escape the castle through the Blood Key door. Mario is fully in the Shopkeeper's control and is ordered to destroy Toad Town. You must kill ten toads (twenty in the original release) to get this ending. A short playable epilogue through another Toad shows the town in ruins and the people slaughtered.

  • Ending 2 - Not Enough Cash!note BAD ENDING - The demon slaughtered you. - Buy the flashlight with 100 coins, buy less than three items, and exit through the Blood Key door. While keeping his soul, Mario has no way to fend off the Shopkeeper's demon form, and after a quick chase, he is killed by it.
  • Ending 3 - Capitalism++note GOOD ENDING - They let you escape. - Buy at least three things from the Shopkeeper (5 in the initial October 2023 release), buy the flashlight with coins, and escape the castle. Almost the same as the second ending, but the Shopkeeper decides to be nice and let Mario actually leave the castle alive and intact.
  • Ending 4 - The Speedrunnernote BAD ENDING - You ended early. - Get to the other side of the intro level and enter the door before the level shifts. Mario is able to avoid being dragged into the corrupted castle... and his reward is being mashed against a wall at high speeds.
  • Ending 5 - Deal!note BAD ENDING - The shopkeeper consumed you. - Refuse to offer your soul for the flashlight. The Shopkeeper loses patience, decapitates Mario, and puts his head on sale.
  • Ending 6 - Painful to Getnote BAD ENDING - Pain was unbearable. - Stand in front of the painful door in the grinder room without the Amulet. The pain becomes too much for Mario to bear, and his head falls off.
  • Ending 7 - Soul and Bodynote "GOOD" ENDING - The demon was(n't) defeated.(.?) - Get the flashlight with either coins or your soul. If you bought it with your soul, you must find the Skull Key in the blue room, but escape the Face Guardian by using the Boo Block to reach the top ledge. This allows the player to get the Amulet, which will let them open the painful door where Mario's soul resides. But you need the blessing of the Angel of Death to actually retrieve the soul (hence the need of the Skull Key to access her if you lost your soul), and the Blood Key. Then escape as usual. Mario defeats the Demon and escapes the castle, but it's made clear at the last second that the Demon still has access to Mario's soul somehow...
  • Ending 8 - The Sistersnote HIDDEN ENDING - She may rest in peace. - Access the floating door in the blue room while soulless, speak to the Sister and bring her three music sheets scattered throughout the castle. This allows you to escape the normal way without the Blood Key, though with a mechanic in place where moving when the music stops kills you. You are directed to fall down a pit and fight the Sister. Mario is forced to kill her, but at least he regains his soul and is allowed to leave.

  • Ending 9 - Mushrooms Consumingnote BAD ENDING - The mushroom consumed you. - Become possessed by the Face Guardian, input the correct sequence of bets in the graveyard to access the hidden Mushroom and eat it. The Mushroom and the Face Guardian don't seem to get along, and Mario's body becomes horrifically warped.
  • Ending 10 - The Trainingnote BAD ENDING - You failed the tr[illegible] - Access the Mushroom, fight it and lose on its first phase. The Mushroom "prepares" Mario for judgement - by cutting off his limbs and ears, gouging out his eyes, and eventually taking his mind, leaving him a near-lifeless husk.
  • Ending 11 - Oppose to the Genocidenote HIDDEN ENDING - You were executed. - Follow the Genocide ending route, but refuse to kill any Toads. The Demon gets fed up at Mario resisting, and puts the player in a Hopeless Boss Fight where it has infinite health. When the player inevitably loses, Mario is executed by the Demon.
  • Ending 12 - Shopkeeper's Realmnote HIDDEN ENDING - You are the shopkeeper. - Travel back up the shaft you entered from using the Cape after getting the Amulet. This places the player in a harder version of the castle that requires four parts of a code to open the shop door and fight the Shopkeeper. Upon defeating it, you play as Luigi in another playable epilogue. Luigi accesses the Shop and finds a catatonic Mario, having taken the Demon's place as the shopkeeper.
  • Ending 13 - The Mushroom's Creatornote HIDDEN ENDING - You are part of [illegible] - Obtain the Amulet, defeat the Mushroom, access the Shopkeeper's Realm and obtain the Mushroom in the grinder room. Possessed once more, Mario is sent out of the castle and towards the creator of the Mushroom, where yet another boss fight takes place. Upon winning, the Creator congratulates Mario by having him "become a part of [him]" - absorbing him into the ground. Note that if you didn't fight the Mushroom, touching it in the Shopkeeper's Realm just kills you.
  • Ending 14 - The Guardian's Spiritnote HIDDEN ENDING - You are one of us. - Buy the flashlight with your soul, then go to the new room in the blue area with the eyeless Rexes and kill them to make your spinjump more powerful. Kill the Face Guardian, then return to the area by the door you need the flashlight for. Spinjump down, and Mario will be taken to a void. To progress further, the purple words hint at the inputs to use. A copy of Mario will appear then have its spine exposed. Hit it three times with throw blocks then grab the key. Mario is taken to a room filled with spine-exposed copies of him, and he is next to be killed.

  • Ending 15 - The Hungry Goddessnote BAD ENDING - The goddess devoured your soul. - Have an intact soul and then make your spinjump stronger. Upon falling through the cracked tiles, Mario will be blown to bits. Mario's soul can move around independently of his corpse for a few seconds before the Goddess takes his soul.
  • Ending 16 - The Fallen Warriornote BAD ENDING - You have been defeated. - Buy the flashlight with your soul, become stronger and fall into the hole. This takes you to a new area with the Goddess, who offers items for sale in return for souls. Leave the room, and the room before transforms and it is now possible to obtain the souls of some enemies you defeat as a random chance. Obtain 5 souls and return to the Goddess's shop to buy the Arena Token. Make it to the Arena, and lose a battle. Mario will be taken to a dungeon that he can escape, but to obtain this ending, let the timer run out.
  • Ending 17 - Spaceship Mechanicnote BAD ENDING - You are lost in space. - Obtain the flashlight and then go into the new dark area to obtain the Arcadeath token. Upon jumping out of the skull-filled room with the token, a Mirror Mario will appear. While having at least a Mushroom, go into the door that contains a hangar for a spaceship, then enter the spaceship. The mirror Mario will sabotage the controls, forcing Mario to eject from the spaceship.
  • Ending 18 - Corruption's Temptationnote HIDDEN ENDING - You are back... ? - Buy the flashlight with your soul and become stronger. Get the Arcadeath token and spawn the Mirror Mario, then backtrack to the main area of the castle and spinjump down to the Goddess's room. To get the Dark Mirror and the warp to the main area, you need a total of 21 souls total to cover both the Mirror and the Warp. After warping back to the main castle, enter the blue door that appeared near the start and long jump into the mirror to reunite the mirror with the original. At this point, defeat the Face Guardian and enter the Arcadeath door. Play the Super R-Type: The Forbidden Level cabinet and defeat the boss. The cabinet will soon display an error message and catch fire, with the keycard now being collectible. Collect it then return to the spaceship, which can now be entered. After one final shump section, Mario will discover that he's stuck in a world where everyone he knows is dead with no way back to his own world. Note that if even one of these steps was not done, you will instead get Ending 17 if you interact with the spaceship.

  • Ending 19 - Succubus Slayernote HIDDEN ENDING - You rejoined the two sisters. - Buy the flashlight and get the Arcadeath token. Defeat the Face Guardian, and enter the Arcadeath. Play the Succubus Slayer Wakana game until it crashes when you defeat the Wakana Mirror. After this, play Sicari and Ditsy Summer Vacation. The letters in red in the messages hint at what the password is to continue Succubus Slayer Wakana. After getting all five hints, get a Game Over in Sicari and resume Succubus Slayer. Clear the level and defeat the boss. This will end with a reveal that the Sister's sister is the Arcadeath's manager, and will cause the two to be reunited, Mario to regain his soul if he lost it and Mario will leave the castle. Note this is the only good ending you can get as Possessed Mario.
  • Ending 20 - The Lonely Mermaid note HIDDEN ENDING - You were lured by the mermaid. - Buy the flashlight with your soul then obtain the Skull Key. After doing so, become stronger and obtain 11 souls. Use these souls for the Fishing Rod and the Warp, at which point you return to the Cemetery. Walk to the pond in the right of the cemetery, and make sure to reel the rod in when the rod is in the red portion of the bar. Mash B to obtain the item, and after some luck you'll obtain the Mysterious Shell. At this point, you can either get your soul back via the Amulet and Death's Blessing or become possessed. Either one will let you access the newly added deep ocean. Finish this area, and Mario will be trapped underwater by the mermaid he freed.
  • Endings 21 through 25 have the first several steps in common. First, you buy the flashlight with your soul, become stronger and obtain 10 souls to buy the Cursed Knife.
    • Ending 21 - Rage of Deathnote HIDDEN ENDING - Your brother made it. - Buy a warp back, grab the Skull Key and stab the Angel of Death. You will now control Luigi as you team up with the Shopkeeper to calm the Angel of Death by defeating it. After she calms down, Luigi will leave the castle, but Mario is dead.
    • Ending 22 - Boss Rushnote HIDDEN ENDING - The warrior comes back. - Stab the Arena Gatekeeper, and he will start a boss fight. After winning, he will let you leave the castle if you defeat all 12 bosses in the arena in a row. Luckily, you have 20 health for the entire boss rush. Once the last boss falls, Mario will regain his soul and leave the castle.
    • Ending 23 - Anomaliesnote HIDDEN ENDING - [no text] - Warp back and stab the Shopkeeper. You will play a guessing game where you have to spot the difference. If there's no difference, talk to the shopkeeper. If there's a difference, enter the door. Some differences are subtle and others are obvious, so you might have to jump in the room to find them. After 5 correct answers in a row, the Shopkeeper will have bled to death, giving Mario his soul back and letting him escape the castle.
    • Ending 24 - Bad Decisionnote HIDDEN ENDING - The gambler executed you. - Warp to the main area of the castle then either get your soul back or kill the Face Guardian, then get to the Graveyard and stab the Gambler. He will kill Mario, and Luigi appears in a brief Playable Epilogue where he will discover Mario's skull is being used for the Gambling Minigame.
    • Ending 25 - Atonementnote HIDDEN ENDING - She atoned. - Stab the Sister after warping back to the main area. You will be taken to a forest where you solve some puzzles to learn more lore behind the Sisters. After this is a boss fight against an Angel that's the sister's sister. Win and Mario will leave the castle with his soul intact.
