Multiple Endings / I Am Alone (Portrait Prophecies)

Following
Go To
Edit PageRelatedHistoryAdd ReviewDiscussionTo DoPage Source
VideoGameNightmareFuelYMMVMultipleEnding…Create Subpage

When the main goal of the game series is to reach different endings, you know you're going to need a separate page for it.

Due to the nature of the trope, all spoilers below are unmarked.
open/close all folders 

I Am Alone 
  • If you quickly flip a light switch several times in a row, this will result in Renée having an epileptic seizure and dying from hitting her head.
  • If you leave the house without the knife through the front door and cross the road, Renée's mutilated body would be found in a back alley next morning.
  • If you take the knife from the kitchen and then leave the house through the front door and cross the road, Renée would stab eleven people to death before getting shot by the police.
  • Entering your bedroom after picking up the knife would make Renée kill herself.
  • After taking the knife, you can also enter the bathroom. This will lead to a visage of Renée's father appearing in the bath. Come closer, and Renée will stab him (eleven times, as the ending text says).
  • Open the front door after you hear the knocking. This will get Renée trafficked by a serial kidnapper only known as the Delivery Man.
  • Getting caught by the shadow on the second floor will result in Renée being killed and dismembered, with her body parts being buried across the countryside.
  • Running away from the shadow and exiting the house through the backdoor will make Renée run to the police station and tell them that an intruder attacked her in her own home. The police are unable to find anything unusual upon arriving at her house.
  • Running away from the shadow and entering Renée's bedroom afterwards will trigger an explosion outside the bedroom, with one of its walls being blown off. Curiously, no signs of explosions are present at the site, as if something just ripped the wall open.
  • The true ending is achieved by discovering all the previous endings in a single run. Going to the father's bedroom after that will reveal a secret passage behind one of the bookcases. Going through it will show you a younger version of Renée in a hospital room, missing her left arm and the ending text offers the true version of the night's events.
